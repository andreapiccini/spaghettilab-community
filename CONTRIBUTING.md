# Contributing

Contributions that improve the public hardware, firmware, software, contracts,
examples or documentation are welcome.

## Licence and provenance

Contributions are submitted under the licence already applicable to the destination
file or component, as listed in `LICENSES.md`. Do not submit code, hardware assets,
models, datasets or documentation unless you have the right to do so. Identify any
third-party material and preserve its licence and attribution.

Every eligible commit in a contribution must include a Developer Certificate of
Origin sign-off:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Create it with `git commit -s`. The sign-off certifies the statement in `DCO` and is
stored publicly in Git history. It is not a copyright assignment or a CLA.

## Temporary copyleft contribution policy

Until a dedicated contributor agreement is available, external code contributions
are not merged into `firmware/` or into AGPL-covered `software/` areas that may also
be offered commercially. Issues, test results, design discussion and independently
written examples remain welcome. Do not submit substantive patches to those areas.

DCO contributions remain acceptable for hardware, documentation, `contracts/`, the
Apache-licensed Protocol SDK and the Apache-licensed Dashboard domain, subject to
review. This temporary boundary preserves the option of a future commercial licence
without claiming rights that DCO does not grant.

## Sensitive and private material

Never submit credentials, signing keys, customer data, private certificates,
manufacturing allocations, unapproved production packages or confidential material.
Security reports should use the applicable private reporting channel rather than a
public issue.

Do not submit release-ready Gerber or drill packages, validated production BOM or
pick-and-place data, panelization files, factory profiles, test-jig materials,
production test or provisioning procedures, final enclosure or packaging assets,
or other controlled Production materials unless a maintainer has explicitly asked
for them through an approved private channel. Public contributors may still submit
source-level improvements, reference designs, reproducible build instructions, and
community-created manufacturing outputs when they have the necessary rights and the
contribution is appropriate for the Community layer.

Accepted Community contributions remain governed by the applicable public licence.
Contributing does not grant official-product status, trademark permission, access to
the non-public Production package, or manufacturing-partner status. See
`COMMERCIAL.md` and `TRADEMARK.md`.

## Dual licensing

The DCO does not automatically grant permission to relicense a contribution under
proprietary terms. A separate contributor agreement is required before external
contributions can enter a dual-licensed component.
