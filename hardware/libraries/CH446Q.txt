A single chip offers 8 Y and 16 X terminals with independently controlled switches at all 128 intersections. It is not a general 24x24 nonblocking matrix. Never join driven outputs with conflicting levels.
Recommended for this project: VDD=5V, GND=0V, VEE=0V; do not power VDD at 3.3V. At 5V supply VIH min is 2.0V, suitable for ESP32-S3 3.3V control outputs.
Serial mode: pin10 P/S low; AX0..AX3 and AY0..AY2 directly to GND; command pins DAT36, CS/CK34, STB14, RST41. RST is ACTIVE HIGH, unlike ESP32 EN. Initialize to all switches off and establish defined boot levels.
NC pins 9,22,23,32,44 must remain unconnected.
RON at 5V and 25C: 120 ohm typical, 185 ohm max. 15mA continuous switch current and 100mA total are ABSOLUTE MAXIMUM limits, not design targets. Do not route power loads through this switch.
An analog switch is not a logic level shifter. External signals routed into ESP32 GPIO/ADC must respect ESP32 limits even when CH446Q is powered at 5V.
Add local decoupling; assess source impedance and ADC settling for analog measurements. 8x16 channel assignment, control GPIOs and actual schematic wiring remain to be defined.
